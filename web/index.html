Staff list

Upload your official list once. When people sign up, press Fill from staff list and their name, province, team and vehicle are corrected to match it — so a mistyped province or team gets fixed centrally rather than chased person by person. Matching is on employee number.

Format 
employee number, name, surname, province, team, vehicle registration, cell number
Only the employee number is required — leave any other column blank and that field is left alone when filling. A heading row is ignored, and you can paste cells straight from Excel.

Service intervals to fix

These vehicles carry a service interval too small to be real — from before the upload started refusing them. This portal quietly reads them as 15 000 km, but the phone app reads them as not tracked by kilometres at all, so the driver gets no reminder. Setting them straight makes both agree.

Bulk upload

Format 
registration, name, current odometer, last service date, last service odometer, service km
Only the registration is required. Dates accept yyyy-mm-dd or dd/mm/yyyy. Leave the last column blank for the standard 15 000 km, or set it per vehicle (e.g. 10000 for a Magnite). A heading row is ignored, and duplicates already on the list are skipped.

Set service interval

Most vehicles run 15 000 km. Use this to put a model on a different schedule — type part of its name or registration, e.g. "magnite".

Set baseline assumes each vehicle was last serviced at the milestone it most recently passed — a vehicle on 85 000 km is treated as serviced at 75 000, so its next service falls at 90 000 and the percentage reads 67%. That gets useful figures out of the mileages you uploaded without pretending a service happened today. Only vehicles with no service on record are touched. Clear undoes it.

Stock, connections and activations are uploaded per team, per network, so everyone in a team shows the same three figures — they are the team's. The tiles count each team once, not once per member. Commission is uploaded per employee number and is each person's own, so it is always summed — as is basic salary.
With All networks chosen the three figures are the team's MTN, Vodacom, Cell C and Telkom figures added together; choose one network to see it on its own. Basic salary and commission carry no network — they are a person's pay, not a figure against a product — so they do not change when the network does.
Somebody with no team on their record can carry no team figures and will show dashes until a team is set on the Employees tab.

A percentage is shown only where both of its figures have been uploaded. It is not capped at 100% — more connections than stock means the stock figure is understated or carried over, which is worth seeing rather than rounding away.

Upload figures

Stock, connections and activations are a team's figures; basic salary and commission are a person's pay. FY has its own tab, and is uploaded there.

Five separate files, one figure each, all monthly. Stock, connections and activations are matched on team name because they are a team's figures, and each row also names its network; basic salary and commission are matched on employee number because they are a person's own pay, and neither has a network.
A team's month needs one row per network — four rows for a team selling all four. A network with nothing to report is left out rather than sent as 0, which is what keeps "sold none" and "not counted yet" apart on the tab.
One file can carry several months — put the month on each row. Uploading the same month again corrects it rather than adding to it, and uploading one figure never wipes another.

Paid to a person, per month, per network — not to a team. Kept apart from the figures above on purpose: FY connections are not the team's connections, so they never reach the conversion percentages or the leaderboard.
One row per person per network, so somebody on both networks appears twice. The conversion is that network's connections over that network's stock.
